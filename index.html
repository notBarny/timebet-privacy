TimeBet — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

TimeBet turns your screen time into a currency you have to win. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: almost everything stays on your phone, and the exceptions are listed below.

No account, no server

TimeBet has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of its own. Your time balance, game history, statistics, achievements, settings and the list of apps you chose to gate are stored only on your device. We cannot see them, and they are never uploaded to us. There is no analytics or tracking SDK in the app.

Permissions the app asks for, and why

PermissionWhat it is used for
Accessibility Service To know which app is currently in the foreground, so TimeBet can hold your chosen apps shut when you have no time left. It reads only the package name of the app in front. It does not read screen content, text you type, passwords or messages, and nothing it observes leaves your device.
Usage Access To count how long your chosen apps have been used, which is what the time balance is measured against. This data stays on the device.
Notifications Optional. Used to tell you when time runs low and when the daily collection happens. The app works without it.

Third-party services

These are the only things that send anything off your device.

ServiceWhat it does
Google AdMob Serves the optional rewarded adverts you can watch for extra minutes, plus the banner and occasional full-screen adverts in the free version. AdMob connects to the internet and may use your device's advertising ID to select and measure adverts. Where the law requires it, TimeBet asks for your consent before any advert is requested, and you can change that choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Privacy options. See Google's privacy policy.
Google Play Billing Handles the one-off TimeBet+ purchase. Payment is processed entirely by Google; TimeBet never sees or stores card details. The app only learns whether the purchase exists.
Google Play Install Referrer If you installed TimeBet from a friend's invite link, the app reads the referral code carried by that link so it can fill it in for you. It contains no personal information.

Buying TimeBet+ removes the adverts, and with them AdMob's involvement.

Referrals

The invite system works entirely offline. The codes are generated on your device by arithmetic and exchanged by you, in whatever messaging app you choose. No server is involved and we do not learn who invited whom.

Your rights in the United States

TimeBet does not sell your personal information for money. However, showing interest-based adverts through Google AdMob may count as “selling” or “sharing” personal information under California's CCPA/CPRA and similar laws in Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas and other states.

You can opt out at any time, from inside the app: Settings → Privacy → Privacy options. Choosing “Don't sell or share my data” there stops your information being used to personalise the adverts you see. Adverts still appear — they are simply no longer based on you.

The only category of information involved is your device's advertising identifier, together with the technical data any internet request carries, such as your IP address. TimeBet itself collects no names, no email addresses and no contacts, because it has no accounts.

Depending on your state you may also have the right to know what is collected, to have it deleted, and not to be treated differently for exercising those rights. Everything TimeBet stores is on your own device, so you can exercise the first two yourself at any time — see the section below — and opting out never restricts any feature of the app.

Children

TimeBet is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13.

Your data, and deleting it

Because everything lives on your device, you are in control of all of it:

  • Reset Progress in Settings erases your wallet, history and achievements.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it has stored.
  • Backup in Settings exports your data to a file you choose. That file is yours; TimeBet does not upload it anywhere.

There is nothing on our side to request, correct or delete, because we hold nothing. Adverts you have already been shown are governed by Google's policy linked above.
